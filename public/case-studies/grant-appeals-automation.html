01 Context

A manual appeals process running on unsupported technology

A charitable grant-making function was managing appeals through a legacy application built on unsupported Microsoft technology. The process was highly manual, which made day-to-day management, approvals and reporting difficult.

Given the volume of requests, a heavy, complex build was not the right answer. The need was for a streamlined process that non-technical users could maintain themselves, rather than another system dependent on specialist support.

02 The challenge
Move a senior, partner-facing audience off an unsupported application without adding cost, licensing or training — and apply AI only where it genuinely earned its place, not for its own sake.
03 Approach

Build from standard tooling, apply AI to one clear task

We designed and built a replacement assembled entirely from standard Microsoft tooling already available across the organisation, with no additional licensing, cost or training required. Power Apps provided the interface for capturing requests, SharePoint handled document storage, and Microsoft Lists served as the underlying data store. Power Automate moved each case through creation and approval, with multiple contributors adding feedback along the way.

At the final decision point, we applied AI to a single, well-chosen task: summarising the accumulated feedback so the approver could make a clear decision quickly, without working through every individual comment. The old system was decommissioned once the new solution was live and in use.
