Most AI projects in smaller businesses do not fail on the technology. They fail because the process being automated was never properly understood.

A process looks simple from the outside. Someone maps the obvious steps and sets up a tool. Then the exceptions start: the supplier who invoices differently, the approval that needs a judgment call, the workaround one person has used for years. None of it was written down, because it lives in people, not documents. AI is good at reading systems and far weaker at reading people, so the tool handles the easy cases while your staff still carry the rest.

The fix is not more technology. It is understanding one process properly first, exceptions and all. Then the right tool becomes obvious, and sometimes it is not AI at all but something simpler. Start with one process that genuinely costs you time or money, prove it, then go further.
