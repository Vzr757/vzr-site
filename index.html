▾040 The blind spot

Ranked search shows you the surface.

Search engines rank what is already well linked. Directories list what someone already catalogued. Both are built around the companies everyone can already find. The long tail, the small, obscure, hard-to-find firms that often make the most interesting targets, stays invisible by design.
